Terms of use

Last updated: November 1, 2026

In short: Driftwood Island is a free, non-commercial fun project. People who follow @driftwoodlive on TikTok show up on a virtual island as residents, under their usernames. You don't need to create an account, pay anything or upload anything to use the site.

The service

  • Driftwood Island (driftwood.rumpuskit.com) is an island simulation that runs in your browser. Driftwood Island is a fictional place. Each resident's home, job and day are generated automatically from the username; they are entirely made up and have nothing to do with the real person.
  • Short videos are made from the island and posted on our own TikTok account. For that, TikTok's developer interface is used only to upload videos to our own account.
  • Driftwood Island is an independent project (rumpuskit). It is not an official TikTok product and is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by TikTok.
  • The site is not directed to children under 13. TikTok itself is for people 13 and older.

Rules

  • Requests written under our videos (like "job: fisherman") only apply to the commenter's own character, and only from a fixed set of options. Requests made on someone else's behalf, or that are misleading or abusive, are not applied.
  • Trying to disrupt the site, overload it or pass off someone else's character as your own is not allowed.

Ads and sponsorships

  • Anything sponsored on the island (for example, a banner on the Driftwood Blimp) is labeled "Ad" or "Sponsored", and sponsored videos are marked as a paid partnership.

Your rights

  • You can leave the island or hide your name at any time: the details are on the privacy page.
  • If you unfollow, you leave the island automatically.

Changes and liability

  • The island, its rules and these terms may change over time; important changes are announced on this page. The project may be stopped at any time.
  • The site is provided "as is"; it is not guaranteed to run without interruptions or errors. Nothing about the lives of the made-up characters is a statement about a real person.

Contact

For any question or request, send a message to @driftwoodlive on TikTok.
