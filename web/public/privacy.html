Privacy and leaving the island

Last updated: November 1, 2026

In short: People who follow Driftwood Island on TikTok show up on the island as residents, under their usernames. The home, job and daily life on the island are entirely made up: they are generated automatically from the username and have nothing to do with the real person. If you want to leave the island, just send a message to @driftwoodlive on TikTok saying "I want to leave the island".

Who runs this?

Driftwood Island is an independent, non-commercial fun project (rumpuskit). It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by TikTok. For every question or request, the way to reach us is a direct message to @driftwoodlive on TikTok.

What we keep

  • Followers: only the TikTok username, the approximate time of the follow and the follow order. These are read automatically from our own account's public follower list, through an independent site that displays TikTok profiles (tik.ninja), by a program running on the project's computer: once an hour only the total follower count (to show who is waiting at the Ferry Landing), and three times a day (9 AM, 3 PM and 9 PM ET) the full list. This is not an official TikTok service. No real names, photos, messages, location or any other information are stored; followers' own profiles, videos and the accounts they follow are never looked at.
  • Comments on our videos: with each move-in (three times a day), the public comments on our own videos (from the last 30 days) are read the same way, only to see whether they contain a request about life on the island, such as "job: fisherman", "hobby: chess", "pet: cat" or "hide my name" (and, while a mayoral election is open, "I'm running" or "vote: @handle"). Comment text is never stored. Only the request itself (username, what was asked for, the comment's ID) is kept for 30 days and then deleted; if you move to the island within that time, the request is applied. The result of a request (your new job, for example) stays with your character on the island. A request only applies to the commenter's own character; replies to comments are not read. On the project's computer, the only thing kept about comments is the comment count per video.
  • Accounts that don't show up: TikTok leaves some accounts (such as private accounts) out of the public follower list. We get no information at all about those accounts, so they can't move to the island.
  • Site visitors: there are no cookies, ads or tracking code. The "on the island now" counter only counts open connections; no IP address or ID is stored. To open the site quickly, your browser keeps the latest copy of the resident list on your own device.
  • "My home" (optional verification): if you type your username on the site, you get a 4-digit code; when you post that code as a comment on one of our videos, it is linked to that username while the comments are read. What is kept: the username and code (at most 48 hours, deleted once matched), an irreversible hash of a random key stored on your device, and the choices you make from ready-made lists (your home's wall, couch and curtain colors, window flowers, your pet's name, the activity for that day and the resident you want to meet; while an election is open, your candidacy and vote). These choices are visible to everyone on the island (that is how your home and your day are drawn on the site). No free text is collected. "Sign out on this device" invalidates the key; when your record is deleted (if you leave the island and don't come back within 7 days, or if you ask to leave), your choices are deleted too.
  • Polls and the hidden object: poll votes and the fact that you found the day's hidden object are kept with a random device ID created in your browser (it contains no personal information; poll results are public, and hidden-object records are deleted after 40 days). To limit abuse, we keep not the IP address itself but a hash of it made with a salt that changes every day, for at most one day. Your browser also keeps, on your own device, the days you found the hidden object, whether you have seen the island tour, and your sound setting.

What we use it for

Only to show you as a resident of the island: your username appears on your front door, on your resident card and in search results. Changes you ask for in a comment are applied to your character. Short videos may be made about life on the island; those videos only show your username, your made-up character on the island and (if you wrote a request) what you asked for. Your comment itself is never shown. The information is never sold, never used for advertising and never shared with anyone.

Where it is kept, and for how long

  • The list is kept on the project's own computer and in a database on its own server. So it can be shown on the site, a public copy of the list is served through Cloudflare; the site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. Verification, choices, poll votes and hidden-object records from the site are stored at Cloudflare (Durable Objects). These providers' servers may be in the United States or in other countries.
  • You stay on the island for as long as you follow. If you unfollow, you leave the island at the next move-in (within a day or two at most); if you come back within 7 days, you get your same home back. After 7 days your record is deleted completely, in both places. The program's logs are also deleted after 7 days.

Leaving the island and your other choices

Wherever you live, you can ask what information we keep about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to its use. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

  • Leaving the island: send @driftwoodlive a message saying "I want to leave the island". Your record is deleted and you won't move in again, even if you keep following. To make sure of that, we keep not your username itself but an irreversible hash made from it.
  • Hiding your name: you can also stay on the island without your username being shown (by message, or by commenting "hide my name" on one of our videos); you then live there as a "Private neighbor". Commenting "show my name" undoes it.

Requests are handled within 30 days at the latest, usually at the next move-in. You can remove your choices on the site at any time from the "My home" page.

Children

Driftwood Island is not directed to children under 13, and TikTok itself is for people 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 lives on the island, send @driftwoodlive a message and we will remove that resident.

The TikTok app

To upload our videos to our own TikTok account, we use TikTok's developer interface. That connection only accesses our own account; no information about followers or any other users is obtained that way.
